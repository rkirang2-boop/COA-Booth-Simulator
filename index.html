About the algorithm

Booth's algorithm multiplies signed binary numbers using 2's complement. It examines Q₀ and Q₋₁: 01 means add M, 10 means subtract M, and 00 or 11 means no arithmetic operation. After each operation, an arithmetic right shift is performed.
